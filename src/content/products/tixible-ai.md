Tixible AI is the applied-intelligence layer for the ecosystem — not a standalone chatbot, but a set of capabilities other Tixible products (starting with Tixible Cloud) can call on directly: summarizing activity, drafting configuration, flagging anomalies before they become incidents.

## Where it's headed

The first release focuses on Tixible Cloud: deployment summaries, anomaly detection across environments, and assisted configuration. Later releases extend the same layer to Tixible PM and Tixible Connect.

## Our approach to AI

We're deliberately not shipping AI features until they clear the same bar as everything else we build: useful on the first try, honest about its limits, and never a replacement for you understanding your own system.
