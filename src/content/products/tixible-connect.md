Tixible Connect is planned as the integration layer that ties every Tixible product together — and reaches out to the tools your team already relies on.

## Why we're building it

An ecosystem is only as useful as the connections between its parts. Tixible Connect exists so that using more than one Tixible product feels like using one system, not five separate logins that happen to share a logo.

## Status

Tixible Connect is on the roadmap, not yet in active development. This page will be updated as work begins.
