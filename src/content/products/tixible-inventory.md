Tixible Inventory is planned as real-time inventory and operations management for businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets but aren't ready for bloated enterprise software.

## Why we're building it

Most inventory tools are either too simple to trust with a growing, multi-location business, or too complex to set up without a consultant. Tixible Inventory is aimed at the businesses in between.

## Status

Tixible Inventory is on the roadmap, not yet in active development. This page will be updated as work begins.
