Tixible Drive is planned as secure, scalable file storage and sharing — designed for teams and businesses, not retrofitted from a consumer product.

## Why we're building it

Every growing team ends up with files scattered across half a dozen tools, none of which share the same permission model as the rest of their stack. Tixible Drive is meant to close that gap using the same account and access model as the rest of the ecosystem.

## Status

Tixible Drive is on the roadmap, not yet in active development. This page will be updated as work begins.
