Tixible PM is planned as project and product management built for fast-moving teams — opinionated enough to set up in minutes, flexible enough to actually stick with.

## Why we're building it

Most project management tools optimize for infinite configurability, which mostly just means infinite setup time. Tixible PM is aimed at teams who want to start working, not start configuring.

## Status

Tixible PM is on the roadmap, not yet in active development. This page will be updated as work begins.
