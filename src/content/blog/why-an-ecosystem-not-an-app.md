Every software company eventually faces the same choice: keep bolting features onto the product you already have, or build the next thing as something new. Most choose the first option, because it's faster in the short term. We're choosing the second, on purpose, from the start.

## The problem with "just add a feature"

A single product that tries to do everything eventually strains under its own weight — the interface gets busier, the codebase gets harder to reason about, and the thing that made it good in the first place gets diluted. We'd rather ship a new, focused product than turn an existing one into something it was never designed to be.

## What "ecosystem" actually means here

It doesn't mean six unrelated apps that happen to share a logo. It means one account, one design system, and one underlying architecture that every product builds on — so using two Tixible products together feels like using one system, not stitching together two vendors.

## What this looks like in practice

Tixible Cloud is the first product built to that standard. Everything we build next — starting with Tixible AI — is held to the same bar before it ships.
